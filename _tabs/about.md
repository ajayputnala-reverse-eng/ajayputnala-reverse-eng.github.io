Hi, I'm Ajay Putnala. 

I am a software analyst and automation specialist with a passion for taking complex systems apart to understand exactly how they tick. 

With professional experience navigating fast-paced production environments and complex software architectures, my technical focus lies at the intersection of software analysis, reverse engineering, and intelligent automation. I specialize in dissecting application mechanics—from memory structures to client-side logic—and using tools like Python and OpenCV to build custom, data-driven automation bots.

### Why "Unpacking the Black Box"?

Technology often feels closed off—hidden behind compiled code, proprietary mechanics, and undocumented APIs. I created *Unpacking the Black Box* to change that. 

The goal of this platform is to demystify software mechanics. Here, I translate high-level theory into practical, hands-on engineering. Whether it is stepping through memory with a debugger, analyzing network traffic, or writing computer vision scripts to automate repetitive tasks, my mission is to provide clear, actionable insights for developers, analysts, and tech enthusiasts.
